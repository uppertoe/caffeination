How the estimate is made

The typical drink is the median of the department’s usual drinks. Small and regular milk drinks are counted as one espresso shot, and large milk drinks and long blacks as two. An extra shot adds one, and a weak drink takes off half. A café espresso shot averages {{ shot_mg }} mg in Australian testing, though single shots have measured anywhere from {{ shot_range[0] }} to {{ shot_range[1] }} mg (Desbrow and colleagues, 2007 and 2012).
